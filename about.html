Our Journey

The story of Ambrosia through the years

2010

The Beginning

Ambrosia opened its doors in a small 20-seater space in Chittagong. Founded by Chef Sebastian Rossi with a vision to bring European culinary excellence to Bangladesh.

First Location 20 Seats
2012

First Recognition

Received "Best New Restaurant" award from Bangladesh Food Critics Association. Expanded to include outdoor seating and introduced our signature tasting menu.

First Award Menu Expansion
2015

Major Expansion

Moved to our current premium location in Agrabad. Tripled seating capacity and introduced our Chef's Table experience. Featured in Food & Wine Magazine.

New Location National Feature
2018

Michelin Recognition

Featured in Michelin Guide as "Recommended Restaurant". Chef Rossi received "Chef of the Year" award. Launched our farm-to-table initiative with local organic farmers.

Michelin Guide Sustainability
2023

Present Day

Now a landmark dining destination with 150+ seating capacity. Multiple awards including "Best Fine Dining" and "Service Excellence". Continual innovation in fusion cuisine.

150+ Seats Award Winning
