Our Story

A legacy of divine flavors since 2010

The Ambrosia Journey

Established in 2010, Ambrosia began as a humble family restaurant with a passion for premium dining experiences. Our founder, Master Chef Sebastian Rossi, envisioned a place where culinary artistry meets exceptional hospitality.

Why We Started

"I wanted to create a sanctuary for food lovers - a place where every meal feels like a celebration of life's finest pleasures. Growing up in a family of chefs, I learned that food is more than sustenance; it's an expression of love and creativity."

- Chef Sebastian Rossi
