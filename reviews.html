Review Guidelines & FAQs

Common questions about our review system

How do I submit a review?

Click the "Write a Review" button above and fill out the form. Your review will be published after moderation.

Can I edit my review?

Yes, you can edit your review within 24 hours of submission. Contact us if you need to make changes after that.

How long does moderation take?

Reviews are typically moderated within 24 hours to ensure they meet our community guidelines.

What should I include in my review?

Share your honest experience about food quality, service, ambience, and any standout dishes or staff members.

Can I respond to other reviews?

Currently, only restaurant management can respond to reviews to address concerns or thank guests.

Are negative reviews published?

We publish all genuine reviews, positive and negative, as we value honest feedback to improve our service.
