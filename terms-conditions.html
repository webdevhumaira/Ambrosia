2. Reservations & Bookings

2.1 Reservation Policy

All reservations are subject to availability. We recommend making reservations in advance, especially during weekends and holidays. We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone for any reason at any time.

2.2 Reservation Confirmation

Reservations are considered confirmed only when you receive a confirmation email or SMS from us. If you do not receive a confirmation within 2 hours of making a reservation, please contact us at reservations@ambrosia.com or call +880 1234 567890.

2.3 Late Arrivals

We hold tables for 15 minutes past the reservation time. After this period, we reserve the right to release the table to waiting guests. Late arrivals may be accommodated based on availability.

2.4 Group Bookings

For parties of 8 or more guests, we require a deposit of 25% of the estimated bill at the time of booking. This deposit is non-refundable for cancellations made less than 48 hours before the reservation time.

3. Cancellations & Refunds

3.1 Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellations made 24 hours or more before the reservation time: Full refund of any deposit
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the reservation time: Deposit will be forfeited
  • No-shows: Full charge for the reserved table will be applied to the credit card on file

3.2 Event Cancellations

For private events and function bookings, the following cancellation charges apply:

  • More than 30 days before the event: 10% of the estimated cost
  • 15-30 days before the event: 30% of the estimated cost
  • 7-14 days before the event: 50% of the estimated cost
  • Less than 7 days before the event: 100% of the estimated cost

4. Guest Conduct

We strive to provide a pleasant dining experience for all our guests. Therefore, we request that all guests adhere to the following:

  • Appropriate attire is required. We reserve the right to refuse entry to guests wearing beachwear, sportswear, or clothing with offensive language or images.
  • Smoking is prohibited in all indoor areas. Designated smoking areas are available outside.
  • We do not allow outside food or beverages, except for celebration cakes for which a cake-cutting fee may apply.
  • Children must be supervised by adults at all times.
  • Any guest behaving in a disruptive, disrespectful, or unsafe manner may be asked to leave without refund.
